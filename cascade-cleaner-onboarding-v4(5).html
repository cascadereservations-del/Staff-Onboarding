The Standards

What "5-star" means in practice. These are non-negotiable.

⚠️ Don't Miss These — 8 Spots Cleaners Most Often Skip
  1. Under the bed — dust and forgotten items collect here every stay
  2. Behind the toilet — the base and hinge area are easy to miss
  3. Top of the refrigerator — guests notice if it's dusty
  4. Inside kitchen drawers and cabinets — food debris builds up
  5. Hair in the shower drain — check and clear every single session
  6. Fingerprints on the TV screen and remote
  7. Light switches and door handles — high-touch surfaces, often skipped
  8. Windowsills and tracks — dust and dead insects collect here

The 5 Non-Negotiables

1
Zero Bad Smell The unit must smell completely fresh when the door opens. Check the sofa, bedroom, bathroom, and trash bins. Use fabric spray on all upholstered surfaces. If a guest smoked or cooked heavily, allow 20+ minutes of full ventilation before spraying. ✓ How to know you passed: Step outside for 30 seconds. Walk back in. If you smell anything bad, you are not done yet.
2
Bathroom Sparkles Chrome polished, no water marks on the mirror, toilet bowl clean inside and out, floor dry. Apply toilet bowl cleaner first and let it wait (soak) for at least 5 minutes before scrubbing. Use a separate cloth for the toilet only — never use the toilet cloth on any other surface. ✓ How to know you passed: Stand at the bathroom door. Look in. Can you see any water marks, hair, or dirt? If yes, clean it again.
🚿 Drain Care & Screen Mesh — Do This Every Session

Clogged drains are the top maintenance complaint at Cascade Hideaway — and entirely preventable. Hair is always the cause.

  1. Remove and clean the drain cover after every session. Pull out all hair using a tissue or your gloved hand. Do not flush hair — dispose of it in the trash.
  2. Check the drain flow — pour a small amount of water and watch it drain. If it drains slowly, use the provided drain-clearing tool to pull out any blockage inside.
  3. Replace the screen mesh protector — this must always be placed over the drain opening before you leave. It catches hair before it enters the pipe. If the mesh is missing or damaged, report to Marifel before leaving.

✓ This is a required checklist item in the Bathroom section of the app.

🛏️ Hospital Corners & Pillow Arrangement — Tap to expand
"Hospital corners" is a specific technique that keeps the sheet flat and tight at the corners. Once you learn it, it takes under a minute per corner.
🛏️ Hospital Corners — Step by Step
1
Lay the flat sheet on the mattress. Center it so there is equal overhang on both sides and the foot. ✓ You'll know it's right when both sides hang the same length and the sheet reaches the foot edge evenly.
2
Tuck the foot end. Push the overhanging sheet firmly under the mattress all the way across the foot. ✓ You'll know it's right when the sheet is flat across the foot with no gap between sheet and mattress.
3
Lift the side overhang of the sheet at one corner to form a 45° triangle pointing toward the head of the bed. Hold this fold against the mattress with your free hand. ✓ You'll know it's right when the triangle of fabric rests on the mattress and the hanging part drops straight down.
4
Tuck the hanging part below your hand straight under the mattress. ✓ You'll know it's right when nothing hangs below the mattress level on that side of the corner.
5
Fold the triangle of fabric still on the mattress straight down and tuck it under. The corner should now be flat and tight. ✓ You'll know it's right when the corner looks like a sealed envelope — no bulge, no flap, no fold visible.
6
Repeat all 4 corners. Then run your hand across the entire surface to smooth wrinkles, pulling the sheet tight before tucking the long sides. ✓ You'll know it's right when your palm slides smoothly across the whole surface with no wrinkles or bumps.
💡 Quick test: After making the bed, press your palm flat on the surface. If your hand slides without catching on wrinkles, you are done. If not, re-tuck and smooth again.

❓ What do you do FIRST when you walk into the unit — before you touch anything?
Tap to reveal answer
Open all the windows and the front door first. Let fresh air flow through the unit for the entire cleaning session. Ventilating first removes odors before you start cleaning, so you are not sealing smells in.
Phase 1 — Arrival (do these first)
  • Open all windows and the front door before touching anything else. Air out the unit for your whole session.
  • Inspect every room for damage or items left by guests. Take photos of anything unusual before touching it.
  • Strip all used sheets, pillowcases, and towels. Bag them to contain dirt and odors.
  • Load the washing machine right away so it runs while you clean everything else.
  • Apply toilet bowl cleaner and tile cleaner now. Let them wait (soak) for 5–10 minutes while you work elsewhere.
Phase 2 — Deep Clean
  • Clean the kitchen: wash dishes, degrease the stovetop, wipe all counters, sanitize the sink.
  • Scrub the bathroom: toilet, sink, shower, chrome fixtures, floor, mirror. The chemicals have now soaked long enough.
  • Dust from top to bottom — shelves, fridge top, all surfaces. Work from high to low.
  • Sanitize all high-touch surfaces: remotes, light switches, door handles.
  • Transfer laundry from wash to dryer.
Phase 3 — Bed & Restock
  • Make the bed: hospital corners, duvet flat, 4 pillows arranged correctly.
  • Restock all supplies — toilet paper, coffee, toiletries, towels. Count and record in the checklist app.
  • Vacuum the whole unit first. Then mop from the farthest corner toward the door.
Phase 4 — Stage & Submit
  • Spray fabric spray on the sofa and soft surfaces. Close all windows.
  • Stage the unit: arrange everything as the guest expects to find it.
  • Check all appliances — see the Appliance Check section below.
  • Walk through the unit as if you are the arriving guest. Check smell, bed, bathroom, kitchen. Fix anything that does not meet the standard.
  • Submit the checklist report. Update the supplies count first, then tap Submit.
  • Send 4 after-photos to Messenger. Then lock the door and leave.
🔌 Appliance Check — Tap to expand
Do this before you submit the report. A broken AC or no WiFi is the most complained-about issue after a guest checks in. Catch it now — not after the guest messages.
🔌 Check Every Appliance
1. Air conditioner: Turn it on for 30 seconds. Make sure it runs and blows cool air. Turn it off when done.

2. TV remote: Press a button. Make sure the remote responds. (Replace batteries if needed — they are in the supply cabinet.)

3. WiFi router: Check that the lights are on and steady. No lights means no WiFi for the guest.

4. Power outlets: Plug your phone into one wall outlet briefly. Confirm it is charging.

5. Report any failure: If any appliance is not working, report to Marifel before marking the unit as ready. Do not close the unit with a broken AC or no WiFi.
🌬️ Smell Troubleshooting — Tap to expand
Odor Rule: After an hour of cleaning, your nose adapts and you may not notice smells anymore. Always do your final nose check at the front door — step outside for 30 seconds, then walk back in. If you are unsure, message Marifel before marking the unit as ready.
🌬️ When the Smell Won't Go Away
Some stays leave stronger odors. If standard ventilation and fabric spray are not enough:

Step 1: Find the source — is it the sofa, the bedding, the bathroom, or a trash area?

Step 2: Open every window and the front door. Run the air purifier on HIGH.

Step 3: Re-wash any fabric items (sofa throw, pillowcases, mattress protector) that are absorbing the smell.

Step 4: Apply fabric spray generously to the sofa and let it dry for 15–20 minutes with airflow before closing the unit.

Step 5: If the smell is still noticeable after 30 minutes of ventilation, message Marifel before marking the unit as ready. Do not mark the unit as ready if it does not pass the smell test.
🧺 Laundry Quick Reference — Tap to expand
🧺 Laundry Rules
✦  No fabric conditioner. It reduces absorbency and causes buildup. Use Laundry Finishing Spray after drying instead.

✦  Detergent: Surf Professional or Surf Matic. About 2 tablespoons liquid or 1–1.5 scoops powder per standard load.

✦  Whites only: Add Oxygen Bleach (Zonrox Oxy) to brighten sheets and white towels. Do not use on colored fabric.

✦  Towels separate from sheets. Always. Mixed loads lead to pilling and uneven wash results.

✦  Dry immediately after washing — do not leave wet linens sitting. Mildew smell develops within hours and is very hard to remove.
