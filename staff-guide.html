Getting Started
The Cleaning App
How to open it, fill it in, and submit your report
What this app does

Every time you finish cleaning, you submit a report through this app. It automatically sends photos and meter readings to the admin — no WhatsApp messages, no separate photo albums needed. One submit = everything done.

1
Opening & Setting Up
2 min
1
Open the app on your phone
Open your browser (Chrome or any browser) and go to the Cleaning App link. The easiest way is to use the shortcut at the bottom of this guide.
💡 Save it to your home screen

In Chrome: tap the three-dot menu (⋮) → Add to Home Screen. It will appear as an app icon — no need to open the browser each time.

2
Select your name
Tap the Cleaner Name field and select Honey Leyson from the list.
3
Set the cleaning date
The app usually pre-fills today's date. Confirm it is correct. If you are cleaning a day after the checkout, make sure the date matches the actual cleaning day.
4
Fill in the guest details
The app may auto-fill the last guest's name and checkout date from the booking system. Confirm these are correct. If they're wrong, just type in the right information.
What to fill in
  • Last Guest Name — the guest who just checked out
  • Nights Stayed — how many nights they stayed
  • Check-out Date — the date they left
5
Select the cleaning type
Choose the type that matches this cleaning:

🔄 Turnover — standard cleaning after a guest checks out
🧽 Deep Clean — thorough deep clean (admin will tell you when needed)
🚨 Emergency — urgent cleaning for damage or special situation

When in doubt, choose Turnover.
2
Submitting Your Report
2 min
1
Review before you tap Submit
When you're ready, scroll to the bottom and tap Submit Report. A confirmation screen appears showing your photo count and checklist completion. Review it — if something looks wrong, go back and fix it.
2
Confirm and wait for the green screen
Tap Confirm & Submit. Keep the app open and wait. You will see a spinning indicator. After a few seconds:
✅ Success

A green screen with a checkmark means your report was received. You are done. The admin has everything they need.

3
If you see a red error message
This usually means your internet signal was lost during submit. Do NOT close the app. The app has saved your work. Wait for your signal to return — the app will retry automatically. Or tap Retry when you have signal.
⚠️ Do not submit twice

If you submitted and are unsure if it went through, check Telegram OPS. If the cleaning card is there — it worked. Do not tap Submit again.

What happens after you submit
1
OPS Telegram — A cleaning card appears in the group chat immediately. Admin and everyone in the group can see your report, meter readings, and meter photos.
2
Email & Drive — A full photo report with all your photos — organized by room — is sent to admin's email and saved to Google Drive. No extra steps from you.
3
Payment card — When admin prepares your payment, a card appears in the OPS Telegram group. Tap ✅ Received Payment when you receive it.

Evidence & Standards
Taking Photos
What to photograph, when, and how many
Why photos matter

Photos protect everyone. Pre-clean photos prove the condition when you arrived. After-clean photos prove you left the unit in perfect condition. If a guest ever claims damage that was already there — your pre-clean photos are the evidence.

A
Pre-Clean Photos — Before You Start Cleaning
📸
Take these BEFORE touching anything
As soon as you enter the unit and before you start cleaning, take photos of the current condition. Minimum 5 photos required.
📸 MINIMUM 5 PHOTOS
Full bedroom view
Living area / sofa
Kitchen & sink area
Full bathroom view
Entrance / door area
Any damage you see
📱
How to add photos in the app
In the app, scroll to the Pre-Clean Photos section. Tap the camera icon to take a photo or upload from gallery. Wait for the upload badge to turn green before taking the next one.
⚠️ Wait for upload before moving on

Each photo shows a small badge while uploading. Green = saved. Yellow = still uploading. Do not close the app while photos are yellow.

⚠️
If you see damage — photograph it and flag it
If something is broken, stained, missing, or damaged, take a clear close-up photo. In the checklist, find that item and tap the flag icon to report it. Write a short description.
Important

Never clean or move damaged items before photographing them. The photo must show the condition exactly as you found it.

B
After-Clean Photos — When You're Finished
📸
Take these AFTER the unit is completely ready
Only take these photos when everything is done — bed made, surfaces clean, everything in place. Show the unit as the next guest will see it. Minimum 5 photos required.
📸 MINIMUM 5 PHOTOS
Bed made — pillows straight
Sofa arranged, surfaces clear
Clean sink, wiped counters
Clean bowl, folded towels
✅
Same angles as the pre-clean photos
Take the after photos from roughly the same position and direction as your pre-clean photos. This makes it easy to compare before and after.
C
Meter Photos — Every Single Session
⚡
Electric meter — 2 photos
Go to the electric meter (outside the unit near the utility area). Take:
📸 2 PHOTOS REQUIRED

📷 Photo 1 — Close-up of the meter display showing the number clearly
📷 Photo 2 — Wider shot showing the full meter with the label and serial number

Electric Meter Reading
2 7 7 6 . 0
Type exactly what you see — including the decimal
💧
Water meter — 1 photo minimum
Same approach for the water meter. Take a clear close-up showing the reading numbers. Type the number into the Water Reading field in the app.
Never skip meter photos

The admin uses these numbers every session to track utility usage and calculate bills. Missing meter photos will flag your session as incomplete.

📱 Tips for good photos
  • Take photos in good light — open the curtains first
  • For meters: hold your phone steady and close enough to see the numbers
  • Make sure the photo is in focus — tap the screen on the subject before shooting
  • Don't worry about perfectly framed "professional" shots — clear and complete is what matters
Room by Room
Cleaning Standards
What "guest-ready" looks like in every area
Our standard

Every guest should feel they are the first person to ever stay in this unit. "Hotel Comfort. Home Warmth." — not just clean, but welcoming.

1
Order of Work
~60 min total
A
Arrive & Assess 5 min
Open windows and ventilate. Take pre-clean photos. Check for damage. Remove all guest items and leftover food. Strip the bed — sheets, pillowcases, duvet cover.
B
Bedroom & Living Area 20 min
Clean from top to bottom — dust first, surfaces second, floor last. Make the bed with fresh linen. Arrange furniture properly.
C
Kitchen & Bathroom 20 min
These need the most attention — guests judge a unit by bathroom cleanliness. Scrub thoroughly. Disinfect all high-touch surfaces.
D
Final Checks & Power 10 min
Check all lights work. Set AC to standby. Replace toiletries and supplies. Take after-clean photos and meter readings. Submit report.
Room-by-room checklist
🛏️ Bedroom & Living Area
Bed made with fresh linen — sheets tucked, pillows straight and plumped critical
Duvet / blanket folded neatly at foot of bed or in wardrobe
Pillowcases replaced — no hair, no stains
All surfaces dusted — bedside table, TV console, shelves, windowsills
Floor vacuumed or swept and mopped
Wardrobe — extra hangers in place, no guest items left behind
Curtains / blinds adjusted properly (guest preference: closed at handover)
AC set to standby / off — remote placed on bedside table
Sofa cushions arranged neatly
TV remote placed on table, working batteries confirmed
Trash emptied and new bag inserted
🚿 Bathroom
Toilet bowl scrubbed inside, rim and seat wiped critical
Toilet flushed and bowl clear — no streaks or stains
Shower / basin scrubbed — no soap scum, no hair
Tap and fixtures wiped dry and shining
Mirror cleaned — no spots or smears
Floor mopped and dry
Fresh towels folded and placed critical
Shampoo, conditioner, body wash replenished
Toilet paper roll replaced (minimum 2 rolls available)
Trash emptied and new bag inserted
🍳 Kitchen
All dishes washed, dried, and returned to cupboard
Sink scrubbed and dry
Countertops wiped down with disinfectant
Cooktop / stove wiped — no grease or food residue
Microwave wiped inside and outside
Refrigerator — all guest food removed, wipe spills
Kettle emptied, wiped outside
Coffee sachets and tea bags replenished
Floor swept and mopped
Trash emptied and new bag inserted
!
Flagging Issues
⚠️
When to flag an issue
Flag any item that is broken, not working, damaged, missing, or needs admin attention.
Common items to flag
  • Broken shower head, dripping tap, running toilet
  • Light bulb burnt out (especially bathroom)
  • AC not cooling, remote not working
  • Damage to furniture, walls, bedding, or appliances
  • Stain that won't come out after normal cleaning
  • Missing item (remote control, kitchen utensil, etc.)
  • Smell that won't go away after ventilating
📱
How to flag in the app
Find the checklist item that has the issue. Tap the 🚩 flag icon next to it. Write a short note (one sentence is enough). You can also attach a photo of the issue.
✅ After you flag it

You don't need to call or text anyone. The flag goes directly to the admin through the app. They will see it immediately when you submit.

Utility Tracking
Meter Readings
Reading meters correctly, every single session
Never skip this step

Meter readings are recorded every session without exception. They track electricity and water usage, which affects billing and helps detect problems like leaks or forgotten ACs.

⚡
Electric Meter
2 min
1
Go to the electric meter outside the unit
The electric meter is located in the utility area outside. It is a grey box with a digital or dial display showing kilowatt-hours (kWh).
2
Take two photos
Photo 1: Close-up of the display — numbers must be clearly readable. Photo 2: Wider shot showing the full meter face.
What to photograph
0 0 2 7 7 6
The digits shown here — type these exactly into the Electric Reading field
3
Type the reading into the app
In the app, find the Electric Meter Reading field. Type the exact number shown on the meter — including any decimal digits. Do not round.
Read the number left to right

If the meter shows 02776.0, type 2776.0 — include the decimal. If there are leading zeros, you may ignore them (so 02776 = 2776).

💧
Water Meter
2 min
1
Find the water meter
The water meter is usually near the water line at the base of the unit or in a small covered pit near the front. It shows cubic meters (m³).
2
Take one clear photo and type the reading
Take a close-up showing the numbers clearly. Type the reading into the Water Meter Reading field. Include all decimal digits shown.
✅ Example

If the water meter shows 54.789, type 54.789 exactly.

!
Common Mistakes to Avoid
✗
Don't round the number
If the meter shows 2776.4, type 2776.4 — not 2776. The decimals matter for calculating the exact usage.
✗
Don't skip if signal is weak
Take the meter photos and enter the numbers even if your signal is poor. The app saves everything — it will upload when you're back on signal.
✗
Don't guess or estimate
If you cannot read the meter clearly (dirty cover, faded display), take a photo of what you can see and add a note: "Meter display unclear — please check." The admin will handle it.
ℹ️ What admin does with meter readings

The system automatically calculates how much electricity and water was used during the last guest's stay, and the average per night. If usage is unusually high (2× normal), admin gets an alert to investigate. Your accurate readings make this work.

Stock Awareness
Inventory Checks
What to check and how to report low stock
Your role in inventory

You are the only person who sees the unit regularly. When supplies run low, you are the first to know. Reporting low stock during your cleaning prevents guests from arriving to find no toilet paper or coffee. You don't need to buy anything yourself — just report it.

1
Check These Every Session
📋
Check all consumable supplies while cleaning
As you work through each room, check how much of each supply is left. The table below shows the minimum levels that should always be in the unit for guests.

🧴Shampoo sachetsBathroom shelf2 pcs

2
How to Report Low Stock
1
Add a note in the cleaning app
In the app, scroll to the General Notes or Urgent Notes section at the bottom. Type which items are low.
Example note

"Low stock: toilet paper (1 roll left), coffee sachets (2 left), no more body wash."

2
If something is completely empty — add it as an urgent flag
If there is zero of something that a guest needs (no toilet paper, no soap) — flag it as urgent so admin sees it before the next check-in.
3
If you see something completely missing or broken
For example: TV remote is gone, AC remote is broken, a kitchen utensil is missing. Flag this as an issue in the checklist section for that room.
